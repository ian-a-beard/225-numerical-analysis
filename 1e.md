The excplit method given by (a) is a 2nd order method which preserves the amplitude of the solution over a long duration, but is subject to phase error. This is observable in the discrepancy between the h=0.08 solution and h=0.02 solution over a long duration.

The implicit method given by (b) has similar behavior to (a). It is a second order method which preserves the amplitude of solution but is subject to phase error over long durations.

The method given by (c) is Numerov's method which also preserves the amplitude. Unlike the previous two methods, it is 4th order. This increase in accuracy significantly reduces phase error. 

Methods (a), (b), and (c) are all symmetric, so that their corresponding polynomials have roots on the unit circle for step sizes inside their intervals of periodicity intervals which are given by $H^2 < 4$ for (a), $H^2 < \infty$ for (b), and $H^2 < 6$ for (c). This explains why none of these three methods experience amplitude growth.
